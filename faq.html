Frequently Asked Nevada Medicare Questions

Answers to the questions we hear most from Nevadans — whether you're in Las Vegas, Reno, or rural Nevada.

Nevada has over 105 Medicare Advantage plans in 2026 — up from 97 last year. Clark County (Las Vegas/Henderson) has 50+ plans alone. Most have $0 monthly premiums. Alignment Health Plan holds Nevada's only 5-star CMS rating.
If you spend significant time outside Nevada, a Medicare Supplement (Medigap) plan is often better — it lets you see any Medicare-accepting doctor nationwide with no network restrictions. Medicare Advantage HMO plans require in-network care, which won't work when you're in another state. A PPO Advantage plan offers some out-of-network flexibility but with higher costs. Compare Plan G vs. Plan N.

However, Medigap plans are available statewide and work with any Medicare-accepting provider. We compare every plan available in your specific zip code.
Medicare Advantage replaces Original Medicare with an all-in-one plan from a private insurer — often with extras like dental, vision, and $0 premiums. Medicare Supplement (Medigap) works alongside Original Medicare to cover out-of-pocket costs with freedom to see any Medicare-accepting doctor nationwide.

Moving to Nevada triggers a Special Enrollment Period (SEP) that allows you to switch Medicare Advantage plans or enroll in a new plan that serves your new area. You have up to 63 days to make a change. This is important because your old state's plan may not cover providers in Nevada. Contact us immediately after your move so we can compare every plan in your new zip code.
Permanent penalties. Part B: 10% surcharge per 12 months delayed. Part D: 1% per month without creditable coverage. Use our penalty calculators to see the impact.
IRMAA is a surcharge on Part B and Part D for higher-income beneficiaries. Nevada has no state income tax, but IRMAA is based on federal MAGI. Selling a Las Vegas home at a large profit can spike your MAGI and trigger IRMAA for two years. See the full 2026 table.
If you qualify for both Medicare and Nevada Medicaid, you may be eligible for a Dual Special Needs Plan (D-SNP) that coordinates both programs. Nevada Medicaid may also help pay your Medicare premiums, deductibles, and copays through Medicare Savings Programs. We can help determine if you qualify.
