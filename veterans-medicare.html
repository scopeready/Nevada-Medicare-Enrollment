What Every Nevada Veteran Needs to Know About Medicare

Yes. TRICARE For Life requires Medicare Part A and Part B. TFL acts as a supplement — Medicare pays first, then TFL covers most of the rest. Skip Part B and you lose TFL coverage entirely.
VA healthcare is not creditable coverage for Part B. If you skip Part B and later want it, you'll face permanent penalties. Many Nevada veterans enroll in Part B as a safety net — giving them access to the state's vast private provider network beyond VA facilities.
With TRICARE For Life, you likely don't need either — TFL already fills Medicare gaps. If you rely on VA healthcare only, a low-cost Medicare Advantage plan gives you options outside the VA system. Nevada's 105+ MA plans include many $0 premium options. Each situation is different — that's why a free consultation with a fellow veteran matters.
Your VA disability rating doesn't directly change Medicare premiums. However, it may qualify you for Extra Help (Part D Low-Income Subsidy) and certain VA co-payment exemptions. Veterans with service-connected disabilities may also have access to additional VA benefits that complement Medicare coverage.
